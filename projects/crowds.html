Crowds Technical Director Internship

Python / Presto / USD / RenderMan

As a Crowds Technical Director Intern on Toy Story 5, I contributed to production shots by integrating large-scale crowd simulations and character animation assets into the production pipeline. I worked across crowd setup, simulation, debugging, and asset integration to ensure scenes remained stable and compatible across a variety of character assets and simulation configurations.

I also developed a Python-based pipeline tool using internal APIs to address character pairing issues in crowd simulations, reducing manual setup time by approximately 90% while improving consistency across shots. In addition, I created reusable character animation assets and animator-friendly rig controls to support scalable crowd workflows and make the system easier for artists to work with.
